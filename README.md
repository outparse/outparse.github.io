# ✨ Outparse

**Outparse** is a free and open-source proofreading tool that helps you write better. It checks spelling (including regional variations), grammar, punctuation, and tone — so your writing is not just correct, but appropriate for the context.

📍 Made in Nigeria 🇳🇬

---

## 🚀 Features

- ✅ **Spellcheck**  
  Detects spelling errors, including **regional differences** (e.g., *color* vs *colour*).

- ✍️ **Tone-aware corrections**  
  Adjusts writing tone — e.g., replaces contractions in **formal writing**.

- 📌 **Punctuation checks**  
  Fixes missing or misplaced punctuation (commas, apostrophes, etc.).

- 🔤 **Grammar analysis**  
  Highlights subject-verb agreement issues, tense errors, and sentence structure problems.

- 📚 **Word database**  
  Linked to a growing dictionary of **37,000+ English words**:  
  👉 [github.com/outparse/english-dictionary-dataset](https://github.com/outparse/english-dictionary-dataset)

## 🛠️ How to Use

- No account required — your work stays on your device.
- Simple interface for quick checks.
- Fast and lightweight, optimized for mobile and desktop.
